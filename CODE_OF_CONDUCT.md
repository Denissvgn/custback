# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss the work, welcome questions, and give
people room to learn. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and disclosure of
someone else's private information are not acceptable.

Keep issues and pull requests relevant to Custback. Critique ideas and behavior
without personal attacks. Share only code and media you have permission to share.

The maintainer is [Denissvgn](https://github.com/Denissvgn). Maintainers may edit
or remove disruptive content, close discussions, or restrict participation.
Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** tools for confidential or
safety-related concerns about activity on GitHub; avoid exposing private details
in a public thread. Software vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
